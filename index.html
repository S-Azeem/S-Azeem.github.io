Treating MOT failure like a probability of default.

Statistical analysis of UK DVSA MOT data: which cars fail, at what age, and why.

About

I'm Shaami, an analytics engineer with a credit risk and physics background.

In credit risk, you estimate the chance a borrower defaults as the loan ages. This project applies the same thinking to cars: the MOT test is the default event, and vehicle age plays the role of loan age.
