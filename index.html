MOT failure through the lens of a modeller

Statistical analysis of UK DVSA MOT data: which cars fail, at what age, and why.

About

Roadworthy is a data project for exploring how statistical thinking can make vehicle reliability easier to understand. The idea is to turn the MOT dataset into a clearer picture of which cars break down, when they do, and whether the pattern is worth acting on.
