MOT failure through the lens of a modeller

Statistical analysis of UK DVSA MOT data: which cars fail and why.

About

Roadworthy is a data project to show how statistical thinking can make vehicle reliability easier to understand. The goal is to turn MOT data the DVSA has for your vehicle, into a probability of whether it will fail its next MOT.
