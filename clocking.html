Odometer Clocking Detection

Status: planned. Method below; results to follow.

The problem

Clocking means winding back a car's odometer to inflate its resale value. Every MOT test records a mileage reading, so each vehicle's test history forms a time series that should only ever go up.

Approach

Readings are ordered by test date for each vehicle, then checked against rule-based flags, similar to first-line fraud rules in lending.

FlagRuleLikely cause
RollbackReading lower than previous testClocking, or keying error
Digit errorReading roughly 10× the previousExtra digit typed
Unit switchMiles and km mixed across testsData entry

Why it's harder than it looks

  • Not every drop is fraud: replaced instrument clusters and tester typos produce genuine-looking rollbacks.
  • There are no confirmed fraud labels, so the flags can't be scored the way a PD model is scored.
  • Instead, precision will be estimated by manually reviewing a random sample of flagged vehicles.

Data: DVSA MOT History.
